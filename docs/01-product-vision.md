# Product Vision

## One-liner

Signal Canvas is the intelligence layer institutions use to detect, monitor, and review digital risks affecting children and adolescents.

## Problem

Institutions responding to online harm need more than blocking tools. They need:

- account and identity correlation across platforms
- monitoring over time, not one-off scans
- multilingual signal review
- structured briefs for human decision makers
- audit-friendly case history

Most products in this space are either consumer parental apps or heavy law-enforcement systems. There is room for a focused institutional review console.

## Stratir fit

Stratir core strengths map cleanly:

- OSINT collection and enrichment
- identity linking across public sources
- watchlist-style monitoring
- AI-assisted risk summarization
- exportable review briefs
- multilingual workflows

Vance's Dark Web threat intelligence background is relevant because harm often migrates off mainstream platforms.

## What we are building

A standalone Tauri desktop app with:

- command-first interaction
- visual signal canvas
- risk review panel
- watchlist dock
- brief export for NGO or CSO review

## What we are not building (v1)

- consumer parental blocking UI
- guardian mobile app
- certified multimodal CSEA classifier out of the box
- full Law 15.211/2025 compliance product on day one

v1 is a credible demo and pilot console. Compliance and domain-specific models can come through partnership.

## Target users

- NGO analysts
- CSO review teams
- public-sector digital protection units
- platform trust and safety partners in Brazil and EU

## Success criteria for v1 demo

1. Looks like a real product, not a Feynman feature
2. Completes a full review flow in under 60 seconds
3. Shows OSINT, graph correlation, and AI risk summary
4. Exports a brief an NGO could discuss in a meeting
5. Supports Portuguese demo path
